Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name and email address provided at registration.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data: pages visited and actions taken within the app, used to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to provide and improve Spendly. Specifically:

  • To display your expense history and summaries.
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To diagnose bugs and improve app performance.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties under any circumstances.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We apply industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls to protect your information. Expense data is stored only for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not integrate with or share data with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Google Fonts is loaded from Google's CDN for typography; this may result in your browser making a request to Google's servers when you visit the site. No personal or financial data is included in that request.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data associated with your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the app.
